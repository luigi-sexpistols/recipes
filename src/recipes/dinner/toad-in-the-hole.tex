\section{Toad in the Hole}

%\index{author!Nagi Maehashi}
\indexbyrecipe{source!recipetineats.com}
\indexbyrecipe{meat!pork}
%\index{country!England}
%\index{bake}

Source: https://www.recipetineats.com/toad-in-the-hole/

Serves: 4

\subsection{Ingredients}

\begin{multicols}{2}
    Yorkshire pudding

    \begin{itemize}
        \item 1 cup lightly whisked eggs (about 4--5 large eggs)
        \item 1 cup plain flour
        \item \sfrac{3}{4} cup full-fat milk
        \item \sfrac{1}{4} cup water
        \item 1 tbsp full-fat mayonnaise
        \item \sfrac{1}{2} tsp cooking salt
        \item Pinch of white pepper
        \item 50g beef drippings, or canola or vegetable oil
    \end{itemize}

    Sausages

    \begin{itemize}
        \item 700g pork or other sausages, preferably 8 small sausages
        \item 10g beef drippings, or canola or vegetable oil
    \end{itemize}

    \columnbreak

    Onion gravy

    \begin{itemize}
        \item 30g beef drippings or unsalted butter
        \item 2 onions, halved and cut into wedges
        \item 1 garlic clove, finely minced
        \item 3 tbsp plain flour
        \item 2 cups low-sodium beef stock
        \item \sfrac{1}{2} tsp Worcestershire sauce
        \item \sfrac{1}{2} tsp black pepper
        \item \sfrac{1}{4} tsp cooking salt
    \end{itemize}
\end{multicols}

\subsection{Method}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Whisk the eggs and flour until smooth. Whisk in the milk, water, mayonnaise, salt and pepper. Refrigerate the batter until needed.
    \item Preheat the oven to \celsius{220} (\celsius{200} fan). Put 50g of drippings or oil in a $23 \times 33$cm metal pan and heat it in the oven for 15 minutes.
    \item Meanwhile, heat the 10g of drippings or oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Brown the sausages on all sides, leaving one side lightly browned and the centres still raw. Transfer them to a plate.
    \item Whisk the cold batter again. Working quickly, remove the hot pan from the oven, arrange the sausages in it with the lightly browned sides uppermost, and keep them away from the pan walls. Pour the batter around the sausages.
    \item Bake on the top shelf for 25 minutes without opening the oven door for the first 20 minutes. The batter should rise and become crisp and golden around the edges.
    \item For the gravy, melt the drippings or butter in the frying pan used for the sausages. Cook the onions over high heat, stirring, until well browned. Add the garlic for the final 30 seconds.
    \item Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute. Gradually add the stock while stirring, then add the Worcestershire sauce, salt and pepper.
    \item Bring the gravy to a simmer and cook for about 2 minutes until thickened. Loosen with a little water if necessary and keep warm.
    \item Serve the toad in the hole immediately with plenty of onion gravy.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Notes}

The mayonnaise helps the Yorkshire pudding rise higher and deflate less. Some deflation after baking is normal.
