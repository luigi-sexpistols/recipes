\section{Greek Chicken Gyros with Tzatziki}

Source: https://www.recipetineats.com/greek-chicken-gyros-with-tzatziki/

Serves: 4--6

\subsection{Ingredients}

\begin{multicols}{2}
    Chicken

    \begin{itemize}
        \item 1kg boneless, skinless chicken thigh fillets
        \item 3 large garlic cloves, minced
        \item 1 tbsp white wine vinegar
        \item 3 tbsp lemon juice
        \item 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
        \item 3 tbsp Greek yoghurt
        \item 1\sfrac{1}{2} tbsp dried oregano
        \item 1 tsp salt
        \item Black pepper
    \end{itemize}

    Tzatziki

    \begin{itemize}
        \item 2 cucumbers
        \item 1\sfrac{1}{4} cups Greek yoghurt
        \item 1 tbsp lemon juice
        \item 1 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
        \item 1 garlic clove, minced
        \item \sfrac{1}{4} tsp salt
        \item Black pepper
    \end{itemize}

    \columnbreak

    Salad

    \begin{itemize}
        \item 3 tomatoes, deseeded and diced
        \item 3 cucumbers, diced
        \item \sfrac{1}{2} red Spanish onion, finely chopped
        \item \sfrac{1}{4} cup parsley leaves, optional
        \item Salt and pepper
    \end{itemize}

    To serve

    \begin{itemize}
        \item 4--6 pita breads or flatbreads
    \end{itemize}
\end{multicols}

\subsection{Method}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Combine the marinade ingredients and massage them through the chicken. Cover and refrigerate for at least 2 hours, preferably 3--12 hours and no more than 24 hours.
    \item For the tzatziki, halve the cucumbers lengthwise and scrape out the seeds. Coarsely grate the cucumbers, then squeeze out as much liquid as possible in a clean tea towel.
    \item Combine the squeezed cucumber with the remaining tzatziki ingredients. Refrigerate for at least 20 minutes.
    \item Combine the tomato, cucumber, onion and optional parsley for the salad, seasoning with salt and pepper.
    \item Heat a lightly oiled grill or frying pan over medium-high heat. Cook the chicken for 2--3 minutes per side, depending on thickness, until browned and cooked through.
    \item Rest the chicken under loose foil for 5 minutes, then cut into pieces if necessary.
    \item Warm the breads and fill them with salad, chicken and tzatziki. Wrap in baking paper to hold them together, or serve all the components for everyone to assemble.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Notes}

The chicken can be frozen uncooked in the marinade. Cooked chicken keeps refrigerated for up to 4 days.
